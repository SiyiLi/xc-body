# North Star

## Product Statement

> Build an embodied OpenClaw agent with continuity, initiative, and restraint.
> StackChan is its home body.

XC Body is not a second generic assistant and not a moving status display. The
OpenClaw agent already owns reasoning, memory, tools, schedules, and channel
identity. StackChan supplies physical presence: face, gaze, motion, voice,
touch, and a stable place in the home.

## Continuity

The physical body should feel like the same OpenClaw agent the user already
knows. A thought begun through Telegram, a scheduled task, or a future Stick S3
interaction may later be expressed through StackChan without creating a second
personality or isolated memory.

## Initiative

The agent may notice, investigate, remember, and eventually offer something
without a direct command. Initiative does not mean constant activity. The agent
should act because a moment is meaningful, not because a timer fired.

## Restraint

The body must know when not to speak or move. Ordinary background work should
usually be ignored, remembered silently, or offered without speech. Private
information must not be spoken into a room. Multiple events should become one
coherent thought rather than repeated alerts.

## Experiential Success

The long-term success criterion is not the number of supported APIs. It is:

> After living with it for a week, turning StackChan off makes the room feel a
> little emptier.

## Device Roles

- **OpenClaw:** mind, identity, memory, tools, schedules, judgment.
- **StackChan:** expressive home body and public physical presence.
- **Stick S3 / XC Buddy:** future portable, private channel; outside Milestone 1.
